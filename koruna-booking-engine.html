The big picture in 60 seconds.

Koruna Assist has a lead flow problem and an admin burden problem. This solution fixes both — using tools already in the CX365 account, without rebuilding anything.

Leads entering through Meta Instant Forms must fill in their details a second time in a GHL form — doubling friction and increasing drop-off. Problems Solved

Three operational gaps, one fix.

Each of these issues was identified in the GHL–WordPress systems audit and the SOP session with Ali.

#3 Critical

Double Form Fill — Friction at the Highest-Intent Moment

"The potential lead will come here and then they have to fill out a form again — it's not ideal." — Ali
Root Cause Meta Instant Forms were built as a workaround because the website couldn't connect directly to GHL. This forced leads to fill in details twice, with inconsistent field mapping between platforms — creating drop-off and dirty data.
How This Fixes It The chat widget replaces both forms with a single conversational flow. Lead data is captured once, mapped directly to GHL contact fields, and passed through automatically — no double entry, no mapping errors.
#9 Structural

Ali's Unpaid Admin — Role-Task Mismatch

"I get paid per sale as a closer… I don't get paid for admin. I've made it as best as possible with very limited time." — Ali
Root Cause Ali's compensation is per-sale. Every hour spent on CRM admin, scheduling, and qualification back-and-forth is uncompensated time. This reduces her closing capacity and creates burnout risk.
How This Fixes It The AI handles initial response, qualification, scheduling, and reminders. Ali's role becomes exclusively: booked call → discovery → close. Lloyd owns all systems and automation from here.
Gap After-Hours

After-Hours Response — Leads Going Cold

Root Cause Static forms and manual follow-up mean leads arriving outside business hours — including broker principals who research after work — receive no response until the next day. Research shows leads contacted after 30 minutes convert at 8× lower rates.
How This Fixes It The widget responds within 1 second, 24/7. It qualifies, engages, and books the call regardless of time of day. Broker principals researching at 9pm are met with an immediate, intelligent response.

CRM Field Mapping

One conversation. Six fields. No second form.

Every answer captured during the chat is stored as a structured GHL field — available immediately for pipeline management, lead scoring, and segmentation.

Industry

Vertical

Mortgage broker, financial planner, tradie, or professional services — tagged on the contact immediately for downstream segmentation and correct pipeline routing.

Team Size

Company Scale

Triggers size-based segmentation. Populates the lead scoring model (2–20 staff = ICP match +10 points) without any manual tagging.

Problem

Main Pain Point

Stored as the "Problem" field. Informs Ali's discovery call approach and pre-populates the objection preparation notes for the call.

Hours Lost

Admin Hours / Week

Used in ROI framing during the proposal. Gives Ali a concrete number to anchor the value conversation: "You told us you're losing X hours a week…"

Service Interest

VA / EA / Loan Processor

Routes the lead to the correct pipeline stage and offer sequence. Flags whether this is a Loan Processor, VA, or EA conversation before the call begins.

Lead Source

Attribution Tag

Automatically tagged (Facebook, Website, LinkedIn, etc.). Feeds UTM attribution — critical for confirming which channels produce qualified leads.
